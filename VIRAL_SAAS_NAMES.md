## 📊 Market Positioning & SaaS Pricing Psychology

Most traditional pronunciation apps (*Elsa Speak*, *Babbel*, *Duolingo*) are perceived as "remedial English tutoring" and suffer from low pricing power ($7–$12/mo) and high churn. 

To build a **$10k–$100k/mo MRR SaaS**, this product must be positioned not as remedial language tutoring, but as:
1. **The Executive American Cadence Engine** (for founders, remote software engineers, creators, and professionals).
2. **The "Gym for Your Tongue & Vocal Muscle Mechanics"** (biohacking & articulatory performance).
3. **The Street Fluency & Verbatim Pop-Culture Shadowing Lab** (learning from authentic YouTube, Netflix, podcasts, and slang).

- **Domain Angle:** `flaptap.ai` / `flaptap.co` / `getflaptap.com`
- **Target Audience:** Tech workers, international founders, and gamers wanting native spoken flow.
